Also, while we wait, what about including additional indicators taken from the field of technical analysis?

  Most classic TA indicators are mathematically redundant with what this pipeline already has. The "technical indicator zoo" mostly decomposes into a handful of underlying concepts: trend
  direction (already return_MA_96), momentum/overbought-oversold (already rsi_12, return_zscore_12, return_sma_crossover — the RSI/Bollinger/MACD analogs from an earlier phase), and volatility
  regime (already volatility_regime_ratio, now also the Hurst exponent for persistence). Stochastic oscillator, Williams %R, CCI are all different formulas measuring roughly the same "where
  does the current move sit relative to its recent range" idea RSI already captures. Bollinger Bands and Ichimoku are built from raw price levels, which this project deliberately avoids (the
  stationary-features rule) — their stationary analogs are already here. Parabolic SAR and OBV are trend-following/volume constructs that substantially overlap with return_MA_96/diff_volume,
  and diff_volume has already shown near-zero importance.

  The one genuinely different concept I'd flag is ADX (Average Directional Index) — it measures trend strength specifically (via directional movement / true-range expansion), which is subtly
  different from trend direction or persistence; none of the current features quite capture "how strongly is this move going, regardless of which way." It's the closest thing to actually new
  information among the classic indicators.

  But given what we've just seen empirically today — MA_192 (highly correlated with MA_96) made both pairs worse, the narrowed cross-pair proxy hurt USD/CHF — I'd be cautious about reflexively
  adding more features right now rather than excited about it. The pattern so far is that correlated/redundant additions tend to destabilize GBT's tree splits rather than help, even when
  well-motivated. Once the Hurst results are in, I'd suggest the more productive next move might be the opposite direction: a feature-ablation pass, checking whether some of the
  already-near-zero-importance features (session flags, diff_spread_close, diff_volume, possibly usd_strength_return) can be dropped without hurting anything — simplifying the model rather than
  continuing to grow it.
