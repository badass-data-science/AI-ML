 Data Scientist – Mindgruve
Mindgruve is a global marketing technology, media, and data company that partners with enterprise brands to deliver predictive marketing and business intelligence solutions. They're expanding their data science organization and are looking for someone who enjoys building practical, production-ready data products rather than focusing on research alone.
This role is centered around taking data science models and analyses and turning them into scalable tools that internal teams and clients use every day. You'll collaborate closely with engineering and business stakeholders, owning projects from prototype through deployment and ongoing maintenance. The work includes predictive analytics, experimentation, forecasting, and marketing and commerce-related data initiatives, with a strong emphasis on building reliable products that deliver real business value.
What You'll Do

    Translate data science models and analyses into deployable, production-ready internal and external tools
    Partner with engineering and account teams to implement and maintain data products used across the business
    Own the full lifecycle of tool development — from prototype to productized, maintained asset
    Work within a growing but still early-stage data science function, helping shape process and standards as the team scales
    Support marketing/commerce data use cases (attribution, media mix modeling, predictive analytics) as they translate into tooling

What We're Looking For

    7–10 years of experience in data science, with demonstrated experience shipping tools/products (not just modeling or research)
    Strong software engineering fundamentals — comfortable owning code that runs in production, not just in a notebook
    Experience productizing internal tools, dashboards, or client-facing data products
    Marketing, media, commerce, or agency-adjacent data experience is a plus given Mindgruve's business, but not required
    Based in (or willing to work from) LA, San Diego, Atlanta, or Dallas
    Comfortable with an early-stage, still-forming team environment — pragmatic, builder mindset over pure research mindset
